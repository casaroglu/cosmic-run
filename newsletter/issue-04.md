## Redbeard

His name was **Hızır**. Europe called him **Barbarossa**: *Redbeard*.

He came from **Lesbos**, a Greek island off today's Turkish coast. He and his brother **Oruç** became **corsairs** in the western Mediterranean.

In **1516** the brothers took **Algiers**. Two years later Oruç was killed, and Hızır took his place. To hold out against Spain, he did something clever: he placed Algiers **under the Ottoman sultan**.

It paid off. In **1533**, Sultan **Suleiman** made him **Kapudan Pasha**: grand admiral of the whole Ottoman navy. A pirate now commanded an empire's fleet.

**Spain** under Charles the Fifth, **Venice** and **the Pope** joined together in a **Holy League** in **1538**. Their fleet was led by the Genoese admiral **Andrea Doria**.

In **September 1538**, the two fleets met off **Preveza**, in northwestern Greece.

On paper, it wasn't close. The Holy League had **far more ships**, with big sailing ships as well as galleys.

Then **the wind dropped**. Doria's sailing ships could barely move. His allies didn't trust each other. Doria **pulled back**.

**Barbarossa did not lose a single ship.**

For about **thirty years** after Preveza, the Ottomans ruled the Mediterranean. In **1540**, Venice made peace and gave up its last ports in the Morea and islands in the Aegean.

Barbarossa died in **Istanbul** in **1546**. His tomb still stands **by the Bosphorus**.

## 3 things you didn't know

1. **The Ottomans once carried a fleet over a hill.** In April 1453, a great chain blocked the Golden Horn. Mehmed's men laid a track of **greased logs** and hauled about **seventy ships** over the hill behind Galata, into the harbour behind the chain.
2. **Don Quixote's author fought at sea.** At Lepanto in **1571**, the battle that broke Barbarossa's fleet, a young Spanish soldier lost the use of his left hand. His name was **Miguel de Cervantes**.
3. **The Ottomans rebuilt their navy by the next spring.** After losing about two hundred galleys at Lepanto, they had built **well over a hundred new galleys** within months. In 1573 Venice gave them Cyprus. Europe won the battle, but lost the island.
