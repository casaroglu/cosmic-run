# Chip Wars 04: The Company That Never Made a Chip (Arm, Cambridge). Fact check (2 sources each)

| Claim in the video | Sources |
|---|---|
| Arm CPUs are in over 99% of smartphones ("almost every smartphone", "the keys to over 99 percent") | Arm FY2025 / FY2026 annual reports (https://investors.arm.com/); SoftBank Group annual report 2026, Arm section (https://group.softbank/en/ir/financials/annual_reports/2026/message/arm) — company-reported figure |

| Over 300 billion Arm-based chips have shipped so far (Arm: 350 bn+ by FY2026) | Arm FY2026 annual report (link above); TechRadar, "300 billion and counting" (https://www.techradar.com/) |
| In 2016 Japan's SoftBank bought Arm for £24 billion (£24.3 bn) | The Irish Times (https://www.irishtimes.com/business/technology/softbank-to-buy-uk-chip-designer-arm-for-24-3bn-1.2725844); Fortune (https://fortune.com/2016/07/18/softbank-arm-acquisition/) |
