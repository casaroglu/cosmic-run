|---|---|

| From two companies, in Odesa (Cryoin) and Mariupol (Ingas) | Reuters via AOL (https://www.aol.com/news/exclusive-ukraine-halts-half-worlds-110634039.html); Tom's Hardware (https://www.tomshardware.com/news/ukraine-neon-gas-production) |
| Mariupol was under siege; when Russia invaded, both plants stopped | CNBC (https://www.cnbc.com/2022/03/25/russia-ukraine-war-laser-neon-shortage/); Reuters via Cyprus Mail (https://cyprus-mail.com/2022/03/12/) ; Tom's Hardware (link above) |

| ...but no chip factory stopped | Tom's Hardware, Micron "we can work around" (https://www.tomshardware.com/news/micron-we-can-work-around); CNN (link above) |

Reworded after checking:
- "In 2022 some prices still rose up to 500 percent" -> "one supplier said prices rose up to 500 percent": the figure is Cryoin's own statement (other sources give ~60% to ~10x by region and grade).
- "A war a thousand kilometres away can still reach your chips" -> "A war far from any chip factory can still reach your chips" (no single distance applies to every viewer).

Map note: Natural Earth 1:50m draws Crimea with Russia (de facto). The video shows the internationally recognised border (Crimea with Ukraine). Coastlines approximate. All pieces (lithography machine, gas cylinders, plants, phone) are generic drawings.
