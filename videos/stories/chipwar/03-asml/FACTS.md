# Chip Wars 03: The Machine Only One Company Can Build. Fact check (2 sources each)

| Claim in the video | Sources |
|---|---|
| The light source fires a laser at falling tin droplets about 50,000 times a second (pre-pulse flattens the drop, main pulse turns it into plasma) | ASML, light and lasers (https://www.asml.com/en/technology/lithography-principles/light-and-lasers); TRUMPF EUV lithography (https://www.trumpf.com/en_US/applications/euv-lithography/); US patent 8969839 (Cymer/ASML, ~50,000 droplets/s) |
| Each hit makes a flash hotter than the surface of the Sun (no number given in the video) | ASML "hot machines" (https://www.asml.com/en/discover/hot-machines); US patent 8969839, laser-produced plasma with electron temperatures of several tens of eV (≈ several hundred thousand K, vs ~5,800 K at the Sun's surface) (https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/8969839) |
| That light prints the circuits on the most advanced chips (EUV lithography) | ASML 20-F FY2020 (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/937966/000093796621000007/asml-20201231.htm); CNBC 2022 (https://www.cnbc.com/2022/03/23/inside-asml-the-company-advanced-chipmakers-use-for-euv-lithography.html) |
| Only one company on Earth builds it: ASML, Veldhoven, the Netherlands | ASML 20-F FY2020 (link above); CNBC 2022 (link above); MIT Technology Review 2026 (https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/06/23/1138837/) |
| Its mirrors come from ZEISS in Germany (ZEISS SMT, Oberkochen) | ZEISS SMT EUV page (https://www.zeiss.com/semiconductor-manufacturing-technology/inspiring-technology/euv-lithography.html); ASML lenses and mirrors (https://www.asml.com/en/technology/lithography-principles/lenses-and-mirrors) |
| Blown up to the size of Germany, the mirror's biggest bump would be about 0.1 mm | ZEISS SMT magazine (https://www.zeiss.com/semiconductor-manufacturing-technology/smt-magazine/so-does-euv-lithography-work.html); World of Photonics (https://www.world-of-photonics.com/en/photonics-industry-portal/detail/euv-lithography.html) — ZEISS's own analogy, repeated by a second outlet |
| One machine ships in about 40 freight containers, 20 trucks and 3 cargo jets (low-NA EUV) | ASML figure via igor'sLAB (https://www.igorslab.de/en/asml-rejects-euv-allegation-no-lithography-system-or-special-components-delivered-to-china/); Asia Times citing Wired (https://asiatimes.com/2021/09/the-150mn-machine-that-is-changing-the-world/); Semiconductor Digest (https://www.semiconductor-digest.com/?p=27277) |
| The newest version (High-NA) costs around $400 million | Tom's Hardware, ~$380m (https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/semiconductors/intel-shares-biggest-unboxing-video-ever-as-asmls-dollar380-million-high-na-lithography-machine-is-installed-in-oregon-fab); MIT Technology Review 2026, $400m (link above); heise, ~€350m (https://heise.de/-11157655) |
| More than half of them work for one chipmaker in Taiwan (TSMC: ~56% of the installed EUV base, said at its 2024 symposium) | Digitimes (https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20241112PD204); AnandTech (https://www.anandtech.com/show/21402); SCMP (https://www.scmp.com/news/china-future-tech/semiconductors/article/3286433/) |
| China has never received one | ASML to Reuters via igor'sLAB (link above); TechCrunch June 2026, ASML "has never shipped an EUV machine to China" (https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/19/); Al Jazeera 2020 (link below) |
| In 2019 the Dutch let China's licence lapse (expired 30 June 2019, not renewed, after US pressure) | Reuters via Al Jazeera (https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/economy/2020/1/6/us-tried-to-block-dutch-firms-chip-machine-sale-to-china-report); DutchNews (https://www.dutchnews.nl/2020/01/us-ambassador-confirms-pressure-to-refuse-asml-export-licence-for-china/); optics.org (https://optics.org/news/11/1/9) |

Notes: coastlines are Natural Earth 1:50m (public domain), shown approximately. The machine, containers, mirror, wafer, tin drops and engineer are generic drawings, not a real product or person. The Hsinchu pin is TSMC's headquarters; the video does not claim the EUV fabs are in Hsinchu.
