# Chip Wars 01: The Town Behind Every Chip (Spruce Pine). Fact check (2 sources each)

| Claim in the video | Sources |
|---|---|
| Chips are cut from silicon crystals grown (Czochralski method) inside fused-quartz crucibles ("quartz bowls") above ~1,400 °C; impure quartz would contaminate the melt and ruin the crystal | USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026, quartz (crucible end use) (https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-quartz.pdf); American Ceramic Society, strategic silica sourcing (https://ceramics.org/ceramic-tech-today/strategic-silica-sourcing); Momentive quartz crucibles (https://www.momentivetech.com/products/crucibles/quartz-glass-crucibles) |
| Most of that high-purity quartz comes from Spruce Pine, North Carolina: analysts estimate ~80% (BloombergNEF, 2024); other estimates 70-90% | ceramics.org (BNEF 80%, link above); TechSpot (https://www.techspot.com/news/102377-) ; Tom's Hardware, Spruce Pine still holds the crucible monopoly (https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/semiconductors/chinese-quartz-approved-for-semiconductor-equipment-and-dram-manufacturing-but-it-still-cant-break-americas-monopoly-china-secures-domestic-supply-for-chipmaking-components-but-spruce-pine-still-holds-the-crucible-monopoly) |
| Its quartz is among the purest on Earth (crucible-grade, used to grow 9N-11N silicon) | Tom's Hardware (link above); ceramics.org (link above) |
| Hurricane Helene hit in late September 2024 and cut off Spruce Pine; both mines shut; roads, rail and power were cut | NPR (https://www.npr.org/2024/09/30/nx-s1-5133462/); Axios Charlotte (https://www.axios.com/local/charlotte/2024/10/01/) |
| Only two companies mine it there: Sibelco and The Quartz Corp | USGS MCS 2026 (link above); AP via ABC News (https://abcnews.go.com/US/wireStory/114699814) |
| Sibelco restarted about two weeks later (10 October 2024) | Sibelco press release (https://www.sibelco.com/en/news/sibelco-restarts-production); AP via ABC News (link above) |
| Others dig high-purity quartz in Brazil, Russia and China, but none matches Spruce Pine at scale yet | Tom's Hardware (link above); ceramics.org (link above) |

Reworded after checking (the plan's wording did not hold with two sources):
- "almost every chip on Earth starts in one small American town" -> "their chips are grown in quartz bowls, and most of that quartz comes from one small American town" / "the chip in your pocket probably started with quartz from one small American town". The sources give ~80% (70-90%) of high-purity quartz, not "almost every chip".
- "the purest ever mined at scale" -> "among the purest on Earth".
