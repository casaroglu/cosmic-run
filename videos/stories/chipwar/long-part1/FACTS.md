Reworded after checking (script lines that did not hold as written):
- Cold open: "The phone in your pocket. The AI you talk to. The car you drive. Every one of them depends on five places" -> dropped the car (many car chips are older-node and need neither EUV nor TSMC); "Both depend on five places".
- "Every chip is cut from a near-perfect crystal of silicon" -> "Almost every chip..." (compound-semiconductor chips are not).
- "The Quartz Corp came back in phases" -> "The Quartz Corp took longer" (the phased return is only in the company's own statement).
- Arm: "Its brain was almost certainly designed by a company that has never made a chip" -> "Its brain almost certainly runs on a design from a company..." (Apple and others design their own cores on Arm's architecture).
- Outro: "a hundred miles from China" -> "less than a hundred miles from China" (130-160 km).
- ⟨verify⟩ lines that held: "mostly neon" (patents + R&D World), "Apple's iPhone chips" (TSMC), the foundry idea, AMD and Qualcomm as customers.
