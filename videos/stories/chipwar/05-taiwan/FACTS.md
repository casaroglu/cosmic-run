# Chip Wars 05: The Island the World Can't Lose (Taiwan / TSMC). Fact check (2 sources each)

|---|---|

| One company there, TSMC, headquartered in Hsinchu, makes them | Britannica, TSMC (https://www.britannica.com/topic/Taiwan-Semiconductor-Manufacturing-Co); TSMC (https://www.tsmc.com/english/aboutTSMC) |

| Taiwan is about 130-160 km from China (Taiwan Strait narrowest crossing; sources differ: ~130 km vs ~160 km), less than 100 miles | WorldAtlas, Taiwan Strait (https://www.worldatlas.com/straits/taiwan-strait.html); Britannica, Taiwan Strait (~160 km); 160 km = 99.4 miles |
| Many call those chips Taiwan's "silicon shield" | IEEE Spectrum (https://spectrum.ieee.org/taiwan-silicon-shield); MIT Technology Review (https://www.technologyreview.com/2025/08/15/1121358/taiwan-silicon-shield-tsmc-china-chip-manufacturing/); Stimson Center (https://www.stimson.org/2025/why-taiwan-fears-america-first-risks-eroding-its-silicon-shield/) |
| America got TSMC to pledge $165 billion for factories in Arizona ($65 bn earlier + $100 bn announced 3 March 2025) | Fortune (https://fortune.com/2025/03/03/tsmc-will-spend-100-billion/); Axios (https://www.axios.com/2025/03/03/tsmc-arizona-chips-investment-trump) |
