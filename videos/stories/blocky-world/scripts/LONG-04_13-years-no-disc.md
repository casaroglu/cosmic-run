**Title:** `I Waited 13 Years for This Game`
(alternatives: `13 Years. No Disc. Day One.` · `I Asked "Is It Ready Yet?" 4,748 Times`)
**Cover hook (4 words):** `13 YEARS. NO DISC.` — picture: Toast holding the open, empty game case
upside down, one moth flying out; behind him a slice of white bread with his eyes, labelled `2013`.
**Chapters:** 2013 · No disc · The floor · The walls · The girls' version · Under the map · 13 minutes.

## Hard rule for this film: our own game, never the real one

The audience must recognise the **launch** (a thirteen-year wait, "best game ever", falling through
the map, walking through walls, a low frame rate, a box with no disc, the network falling over on day
one, and the question "what's the girls' version?"), never a trademark. The game is
**MEGA DONUT HEIST 2**, the same invented game as in Short T5. It runs on the **Turbo City** server
from Long 2; Short T5's Glaze City street corner is its spawn point at the edge of the avenue. No real
title, logo, cover layout, map, character, car, console, store or button symbol is shown or imitated.
The controller is The Admin's white gamepad with our icons (donut, toast, star, skateboard). The
"heist" is a vault of donuts and the criminal is a hungry pet. No song lyrics: the in-game radio is an
instrumental.

## Spine

- **Want:** Toast pre-ordered the game in 2013, when he was still a slice of bread. He wants to play
  it tonight, and for once to have the glowing `MAIN CHARACTER` arrow over his own head (in Long 2 he
  was a `GUEST` and the main characters never looked at him).
- **Device (drives every scene):** the **patch bar**, stuck at `99%`, with a `TIME LEFT` beside it, and
  the red **`IS IT READY YET?`** button (the star button on the controller). The rule is the yellow
  sticker on the box: `DAY-ONE PATCH REQUIRED. DO NOT PLAY BEFORE 100%.`
  Every time Toast will not wait, he asks or he plays anyway, a small `+1` floats up, the time left gets
  longer (`13 MINUTES` → `13 HOURS` → `13 DAYS` → `13 YEARS`) and the game breaks one size bigger.
- **Engine:** each scene is one wait, one shortcut, one bug. The Admin answers each complaint with a
  patch note that fixes that thing and breaks the next one.
- **Pixi is right four times:** "Wait for the patch." · "You've pressed that button every day for
  thirteen years." · "It's the same game." · "See."
- **Twist:** the game was due the day after Toast pre-ordered it. The Admin makes it alone, with one
  finger, and has to stop and answer every time somebody asks if it is ready. Each ask costs one day.
  Toast has asked 4,748 times. The thirteen-year wait is Toast.
- **What Toast learns (one thing):** he sits on his hands for thirteen minutes and does not ask.

## Sound notes

- Never whoosh, swish, zip or riser. Falls are a `pop` at the top and a `thud` at the bottom, with
  nothing between. Typing, countdowns and the slideshow are `tick`s at one fixed pitch.
- The patch chip's `tick` (one every 0.4 s, very low in the mix) runs under every scene from scene 2
  beat 5 and stops only twice: in the slideshow (replaced by one `tick` per still) and at `100%`.
- The `+1` has its own small `pop`, always the same, so that scene 6 can replay it.
- The workshop has no music: the lamp hum and one finger on a keyboard.
- Meme cues at least 3 s apart: boom 512389 (cold open, loop) only.
- **TOASTED!** twice (scene 4 beat 9, scene 6 beat 8), bible look; the first one arrives as a held
  still. CRASH at most twice per scene.
- Toast's "Fu—" (own recording, cut at 0.4 s) once, on the last frame.
- Voices: Bread Toast is Toast, higher and softer; the Question Kid is a pitched Kokoro take. Toast and
  Pixi lines are full sentences; nobody says a frame-rate abbreviation out loud.

## Notes for the build

- This film must not repeat Short T5. There, the joke is "only the road is finished" and the loop is
  the tile failing. Here the falls are one beat each (scene 3), the slideshow is nine seconds
  (scene 4), and the film's own ideas are the bread, the `+1`, the patch notes, Donutsaurus as a
  vehicle, scene 5 and the workshop.
- The number on the wall counter is 4,751: 4,748 days from 19 November 2013 to 19 November 2026 plus
  tonight's three presses (scene 2 beat 6, scene 3 beat 7, scene 4 beat 10). Keep those three and no more.
- Do not explain the `+1` before scene 6. In scene 1 it is small, white and gone in half a second.
- The main characters stay far away and in silhouette; the bicep heart must read as a shape.
- Pixi is never in danger and never falls through anything, in any version of the game.
- Build order: home scenes (2, 7, cold open) and scene 1's recolours first; then scene 3 on the T5 set;
  then the avenue scenes (4, 5); the workshop (6) last.
- Staging guard on every scene; `skip` for Toast-in-a-wall, Toast on Donutsaurus and the stamp.
