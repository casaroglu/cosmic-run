**Title:** `I Queued All Night for a $9,999 Mystery Box`
(alternatives: `Everyone Has a Zubbit Except Me` · `The Mystery Box Was Worth $9,999 Until 3 AM`)
**Cover hook (4 words):** `WORLD'S BIGGEST BAG CHARM` — picture: the loop pose (Donutsaurus hanging from
Toast's backpack by a giant clip, Toast tipping over backwards), a tiny grey Zubbit in the corner for scale.
**Chapters:** Everyone has one · The line · The drop · Friend price · The jumbo · 3 AM.

## Hard rule for this film: our own craze, never the real one

The audience must recognise the **situation** (a blind box, a toothy plush on every bag, an all-night
line, one rare "chase" figure, resale prices, a reseller, a badly made fake with a misspelled name, and
the morning the craze ends), never a trademark. No real toy, shop, brand, app or person is named, shown
or imitated. The plush is the Zubbit: grey rounded cube, **one** square buck tooth, three stubby horns,
zip-pull tail, carabiner. If a prop starts to look like a real product, change its colour and silhouette.
No song lyrics; the line's radio is an instrumental.

## Spine

- **Want:** The Admin banned Toast's weekends at the end of Long 2. They are now on the price board:
  `TOAST'S WEEKENDS $9,999`. A Gold Zubbit resells for exactly `$9,999`. Toast wants one Gold Zubbit.
- **Device (drives every scene):** the **ticker**, an LED board that hangs in the shop and over the
  shop door: `GOLD ZUBBIT — RESALE $9,999 ▲`, and under it, always readable:
  `WORTH = WHAT THE NEXT PERSON PAYS`. In every scene Toast pays more for something worth less.
  A receipt line pops under the HUD each time: `PAID …` / `WORTH …`.
- **The Admin's rule (already known from Short T6):** `LIMIT: 1 BOX PER PERSON`. Toast's one box is
  gone in scene 1, so the whole film is him trying to get a Gold one some other way.
- **Engine:** Toast looks at the price. Donutsaurus looks at the bags. Toast wants a Zubbit to sell;
  Donutsaurus wants to **be** somebody's bag charm. Pixi wants nothing and has two dollars.
- **Pixi is right four times:** "Don't buy it. It's a box." · "They never call that one." · "It's fake."
  · "You have a monster."
- **Twist:** the craze had an end time printed on the poster all along (`CRAZE ENDS FRIDAY 3:00 AM`).
  The Admin prices everything by his own rule, so when the Gold Zubbit falls to `$2`, Toast's weekends
  fall to `$2` too. Toast has `$0`. Pixi has two dollars.
- **What Toast learns (one thing):** some things you do not sell. He says the same sentence twice,
  "He's a responsibility", and it means the opposite the second time.
