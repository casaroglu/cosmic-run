# Long video 2 — "We Joined the WRONG Servers" (about 9 min, 1920×1080)

Read `../BIBLE.md` first. Story by the user (2026-11-25): our blocky heroes leave their own universe, land in a
superhero finale where the villain snaps and everyone vanishes except them, then in a cube world where the
mascot eats an exploding monster, then in a crowded crime-city street, and go home through a game controller's
pause menu ("Return to My Universe").

## Hard rule for this film: parody worlds, never the real ones

The three worlds are **genres the audience knows at first sight**, drawn with our own characters, names and
shapes. Do not use or imitate the real names, logos, costumes, faces or symbols of any existing film, game or
console (no real hero or villain, no real game mob, no real game characters, no console logo or its button
symbols). If a design starts to look like a real character, change its colour, silhouette and prop.
Why it still works: kids recognise the *moment* (the snap, the hiss before the boom, the wanted level, the pause
menu), not the trademark.

## Spine

- **Want:** Toast is "night manager forever" (end of Long 1). He wants one night off.
- **Device:** The Admin's game controller, left in the lost-and-found box with a note: `ADMIN'S. DO NOT TOUCH.`
  It has a battery of three bars. **Every jump costs one bar.**
- **Engine:** each world has one physics rule that our three do not fit. They are tagged `GUEST` in every world.
- **Set-ups that pay off later:** the swallowed magnet (Cape City → home), the explosion inside Donutsaurus
  (Cubelands → charges the controller in Turbo City), the confetti in Toast's hood (Cape City → final twist).
- **Twist:** they did not leave the heroes behind. They brought them home.

## The look of each world (this is the film's main visual idea)

Each universe has its own picture style and its own game HUD. The moment they land, the HUD changes.

| World | Picture | HUD | Physics rule | Music |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home: Donut Planet | Pink shop, as in Long 1 | `NIGHT 8`, coin counter | Rules on the wall | `mischief` |
| Cape City | Dusk skyline, rubble, comic-book halftone dots in the shadows; impact words as 2D bursts (`POW`, `KA-CLANG`) | Comic caption boxes in yellow; a `FINAL BATTLE` bar across the top with the villain's health | Everybody has a power. Guests have none | `epic` |
| Cubelands | Everything is 1 m cubes: square sun, square clouds, cube trees, flat green ground | A hotbar of 9 slots at the bottom, 10 hearts, a day/night dial | Nothing falls when you remove what is under it. Night comes in three seconds | `happy`, then `tense` |
| Turbo City | Neon sunset, palm trees, six-lane avenue, wet asphalt reflections | Round minimap bottom-left, `ALARM` meter of five donuts top-right, a cash number | The smallest offence raises the alarm. Traffic never stops | `tense` |

Transition between worlds, always the same: a full-screen loading card, `JOINING SERVER…` with a bar that
sticks at 99%, a spinning donut, and a **loading tip** that is a joke (written in each scene below).
Above our three characters, in every foreign world, a small grey tag: `GUEST`.

## Notes for the build

- Order: build Home (exists), then Cubelands (cheapest: only cubes), then Turbo City, then Cape City (most new
  characters). Render scenes separately and join.
- The confetti puff, the flipping tiles and the loading card are reused in every scene: build them once.
- The freeze in Turbo City: stop every animated value except our three, drop saturation to 20%, hold the cars in
  mid-air. The pause menu is a 2D overlay; the cursor is Toast's real hand in front of the camera.
- The 4 silent seconds after the snap are the centre of the film. No music, only wind.
- Keep the three hidden set-ups small: the pink glow in Donutsaurus's belly (one frame in Cape City, steady in
  Turbo City), the confetti entering the hood, the `EXPLOSION ×1 (stored)` hotbar item.
- Voices for the new characters are suggestions in Kokoro terms; if the voice engine changes, keep the
  descriptions (deep and calm, heroic, old and slow, very fast).
- Chapters: The box · Cape City · Cubelands · Turbo City · Home.
- Thumbnail idea: the red oven mitt mid-snap, small Toast in front, text `WRONG SERVER`.

## Three more long-video outlines for the same audience

**"100 Players, 1 Donut" (Drop Island).** A hundred players jump from a flying bus onto an island; the last one
standing wins the Golden Donut. Toast forgets his parachute and lands first, in a crater. The storm circle
closes; the counter `PLAYERS LEFT` drops in every scene without Toast doing anything: they knock each other out
while he hides in a bush, falls down a hill, gets stuck in a door. Pixi plays properly and reaches the final
two. Twist: the bush Toast hid in all game was Donutsaurus, who eats the Golden Donut. `WINNER: THE BUSH`.

**"I Built a Donut Empire With 1 Coin" (tycoon).** Toast has exactly one coin (from Long 1). He buys one donut,
sells it for two, and the tycoon counter starts: stand, shop, factory, tower, a rocket. Every upgrade is a
bigger, sillier machine, and every machine has one obvious flaw that he ignores. The Admin appears each time
the number gets big, with a new tax. At the top Toast owns everything and has no time to eat a donut. He sells
it all back for one coin, buys one donut, and shares it. Running counter on screen the whole film.

**"Escape the Admin's Obby" (obstacle course).** Toast is banned into a tower of twenty stages; each stage is a
thing The Admin has banned (floor, gravity, colour, sound, the letter T). Pixi runs it in ninety seconds on a
split screen while Toast is still on stage two. Each stage is one physical gag and one stamp. At the top is a
door marked `EXIT`; behind it is stage one. Twist: Pixi built the obby as his training. `LESSON 3: EVERYTHING`.
