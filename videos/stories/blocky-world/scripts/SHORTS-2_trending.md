# Toast & Pixi — Shorts scripts (set 2: trending, late November 2026)

Read `../BIBLE.md` first. Camera names, gags and limits are defined there. Table format is the one in
`SHORTS.md`: each row is one shot, "Dur" is seconds, lines are final and recorded exactly.
Six vertical Shorts (1080×1920), one trend each. Build order: T2, T1, T6, T5, T3, T4
(T2 needs no new rig work; T5 is the only one with a new set).

**Rules for this set (where they differ from set 1, this file wins):**

- **Loop:** the last spoken line is *finished*, not cut. Then a boom (`impact`), the picture hard-cuts to
  the hook's exact composition (shot 1: same camera, poses, props — one shared pose object), and Toast
  says `"Fu—"`, cut before the word is a word. The "Fu—" shot is the last row of every table and is the
  same picture as row 1 without the hook text.
- **Parody only.** No real brand, game, person, logo or character appears or is named. Every name below
  is invented; do not "correct" one to a real name. No song lyrics, no music that imitates a real track.
- **Sound:** only the names used in `SHORTS.md` (`sting`, `boing`, `pop`, `ding`, `tick`, `thud`,
  `impact`, `crash`, `stamp`, `click`, `bell`, `magic`, `door`, `footsteps`) plus laughs and burps from
  `sfx_lib/library.json`. Never whoosh, swish, zip or riser.
- **Extra voices:** kids and customers (T1, T4) have no lines of their own except where written; make the
  kid chorus from three takes of the line in any Kokoro voice pitched +7 st and layered.
- **Mute test:** every rule is a physical sign, every escalation has a counter, every twist is an INSERT
  on small print. The small print must be unreadable in WIDE and MED until its INSERT.

- **Name:** **ZUBBIT** (plural Zubbits). Sold in sealed **Zubbit Mystery Boxes** from a vending machine.
  Check the name for an existing trademark before the first upload; if it has to change, change it in
  both scripts.
- **Design:** a fist-sized blocky plush. Body is a rounded cube, slightly wider than tall. **One** big
  square buck tooth in the middle of a straight-line mouth (never a row of pointed teeth). Two round
  button eyes set far apart. **Three** short stubby horns in a row on top (no long ears). A short tail
  that ends in a zip-pull. Hangs from a chunky plastic carabiner clip on top of the head.
- **Colours:** common = **Plain Zubbit**, flat grey with a white tooth. Chase = **Gold Zubbit**, gold
  body, the tooth is a tiny glazed donut, odds printed on the poster: `1 IN 144`.

- **Fakes (for the long video; not used in T6):** the name is misspelled `ZUBBLT`, two teeth instead of
  one, horns of different lengths.
