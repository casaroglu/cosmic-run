## S1 — Night Shift #1: "Don't Feed the Mascot" (30 s)

**Set:** Donut Planet (existing shop). Remove the toaster and the customer. Add: a pet bed with a bowl

**Rule sign** (on the wall beside the counter, big): `NIGHT SHIFT RULE #1: DO NOT FEED THE MASCOT AFTER 3 AM`

wide and medium shots.
**Cast:** Toast, Pixi, Donutsaurus (starts at scale 0.35, "puppy").

## S5 — Ragdoll School, Lesson 1: "Stairs" (30 s)

**Idea:** the simplest task there is, failed five different ways. A numbered fail list.
**Set:** new, small: a staircase of 8 steps going down from left to right against a plain wall, a railing,
an elevator door at the top next to the stairs, a soft landing mat at the bottom. A sign at the top:
`RAGDOLL SCHOOL — LESSON 1: STAIRS`. Donutsaurus sits at the bottom next to the mat, watching.
**Counter:** `ATTEMPT 1` … `ATTEMPT 5`, each with a `ding`. After each fall a red stamp `FAIL`.
**Cast:** Pixi (teacher, with a pointer stick), Toast, Donutsaurus.

| # | Dur | Camera | Action | Line | Text / gag | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1.5 | WIDE | Flash-forward: Toast spinning through the air above the stairs, limbs everywhere | TOAST: "Aaaah!" | `HOW TO WALK DOWN STAIRS` | `sting` |

one fall animation.
