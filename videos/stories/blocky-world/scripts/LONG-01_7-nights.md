# Long video 1 — "I Survived 7 Nights at Donut Planet" (about 8.5 min, 1920×1080)

Read `../BIBLE.md` first. This is seven rule-break stories with one thread through them.

## Spine

- **Want:** Toast broke Pixi's skateboard. A new one costs 700 coins. Donut Planet pays 100 coins a night.
- **Obstacle:** every night has one rule. Toast breaks it, and the damage comes out of his pay.
- **Thread:** somebody keeps adding a new rule to the sign each night. Who?
- **Change:** in Night 7 Toast finally uses the six rules correctly. That is the only time he wins.
- **Twist:** the skateboard was fixed on Night 1. The Admin owns the shop. Toast gets promoted, which is worse.

## Always on screen (HUD)

- Top left: `NIGHT 3 / 7`. Top right: a coin and `COINS: 20`. The number rolls when it changes (`coin`).
- Each night opens with a 1.5 s title card: `NIGHT 3` and the rule in quotes, read by The Admin.
- Each night ends with the pay slip: `PAY +100` then `DAMAGE −NN` then the new total. Toast's face falls.
- The clock on the wall always shows the real story time. Every night starts at 2:59.
- The rule sign grows by one line each night. Old rules stay visible.

## Sets and cast

Donut Planet only (front area, counter, door, back wall). Added per night: wall phone (N2), light switch (N3),
order microphone (N4), a queue rope and a flashlight (N5), a walk-in freezer door on the right wall (N6).
Cast: Toast, Pixi, Donutsaurus, The Admin (hand and voice), customers (the grey blocky customer, recoloured
three ways), Burntosaurus (N4, N7).

Pay table (keep these numbers): N1 −90 → 10 · N2 −80 → 30 · N3 −95 → 35 · N4 −100 → 35 · N5 −0 → 135 ·
N6 −99 → 136 · N7 +100, final damage bill −235 → **1 coin**.

## Notes for the build

- Night 1 reuses Short S1's assets. Build S1 first.
- Nights are separate projects that share one set file; render them separately and join, so one night can be
  fixed without re-rendering the film.
- The rule sign and the HUD are the spine of the film. They must be readable in every night's first shot.
- Three clues for the twist, never pointed at: Pixi rides a board at the end of Night 1; a taped skateboard
  leans by the door in Night 4's photo beat; Pixi arrives "by board" sound (rolling wheels: `footsteps` is wrong,
  use a soft `tick` roll) in Night 6.
- Chapters for the description: Cold open · Night 1 … Night 7 · The test.
- Thumbnail idea: Toast between two giant mascots, text `7 NIGHTS`.
