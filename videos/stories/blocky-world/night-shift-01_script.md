# Night Shift #1 — "The Customer With No Shadow" (pilot, ~38 s)

Cast: Toast, Pixi (walkie-talkie, then in the shop), Donutsaurus, the Customer With No Shadow.
Set: "Donut Planet" shop at night. Hidden 1 s text on the wall: NIGHT SHIFT RULE #1: Never serve a customer with no shadow.

Sound rule: no whoosh/swish/zip/riser; zooms use boing or sting.
