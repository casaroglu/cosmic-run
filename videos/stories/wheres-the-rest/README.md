## 01 Oliver Cromwell's head (45.8 s)
- Output: `~/cizgi/bodyparts-01-cromwell/out/Wheres_The_Rest_01_-_Cromwell.mp4` (+ `_small.mp4`), cover `01-cromwell_cover.jpg` (Gemini, hook HIS HEAD WENT ON TOUR).
- Facts (two sources each: Wikipedia "Oliver Cromwell's head", History.co.uk, Mental Floss): died 1658; exhumed, hanged at Tyburn and beheaded 30 Jan 1661; head on a pole on Westminster Hall until at least 1684; later owners Claudius Du Puy (private museum, by 1710), Samuel Russell (failed actor), Cox (jeweller), Hughes brothers (1799 exhibition), Wilkinson family from 1815; buried at Sidney Sussex College, Cambridge, 25 March 1960, exact spot secret.
- Images (PD/CC0, Wikimedia Commons): Samuel Cooper Cromwell portrait (after 1656); Microcosm of London plates 94 and 103 (Westminster Hall, 1810); Hogarth, The Idle 'Prentice Executed at Tyburn (1747, NGA, CC0); Loggan, Sidney Sussex College (1690).
