# Chokepoints 3: the Panama Canal — fact check

| Claim | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Panama has owned the canal since 1999 | ✓ handover at noon, 31 Dec 1999 | History.com "Panama takes control of Panama Canal"; Wikipedia "Panama Canal" |
| Crosses near the narrowest point, Colón to Panama City | ✓ Atlantic entrance at Colón, Pacific at Panama City/Balboa | Britannica "Panama Canal"; Wikipedia |
| 82 km of locks, hills and an artificial lake | ✓ ~82 km; Gatun Lake, Culebra Cut, three lock sets | Britannica; Wikipedia |
| New York–San Francisco via Cape Horn; canal cuts the trip by more than half | ✓ ~20,900 km around the Horn vs ~8,400 km through the canal | Wikipedia "Panama Canal"; history.state.gov |
| France tried first, 1881, under Ferdinand de Lesseps (Suez) | ✓ work began 1881 | history.state.gov "Building the Panama Canal"; Wikipedia |
| Yellow fever and malaria killed about 20,000; company bankrupt 1889 | ✓ ~22,000 est.; liquidation Feb 1889 | Wikipedia "French construction attempts"; history.state.gov |
| 1903 Panama broke from Colombia, backed by US warships; US got the Canal Zone | ✓ independence 3 Nov 1903; USS Nashville; Hay–Bunau-Varilla Treaty, 10-mile zone | history.state.gov; Britannica "Hay–Bunau-Varilla Treaty" |
| Americans fought mosquitoes, cut through mountains; about 5,600 died | ✓ ~5,609 deaths (1904–1914 records) | History.com; Wikipedia |
| 15 Aug 1914 the Ancon made the first official crossing | ✓ SS Ancon, 15 Aug 1914 | Wikipedia "Panama Canal"; Britannica |
| 1977 Carter and Torrijos signed treaties to hand it over | ✓ Torrijos–Carter Treaties, 7 Sep 1977 | history.state.gov; Britannica |
| Each ship uses millions of litres of lake water; in a drought fewer ships pass | ✓ ~200 million litres per transit (older locks); 2023–24 drought cut daily transits | Panama Canal Authority; Reuters/AP 2023–24 coverage |
| 2025 Trump said the US would take it back; Panama: it stays Panamanian | ✓ inaugural address 20 Jan 2025; President Mulino's reply | gCaptain; CBC; The Maritime Executive |
