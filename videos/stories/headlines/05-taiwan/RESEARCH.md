# Chokepoints 5: the Taiwan Strait — fact check

| Claim | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| About 130 km of water between China and Taiwan at the narrowest | ✓ ~126–130 km (Pingtan–Hsinchu) | Wikipedia "Taiwan Strait"; Britannica |
| Links the East and South China Seas | ✓ | Wikipedia; Britannica |
| Austronesian peoples first; Dutch built Fort Zeelandia in 1624 | ✓ | Wikipedia "Fort Zeelandia"; Britannica "Taiwan" |
| Koxinga besieged it in 1661; Dutch surrendered early 1662 | ✓ treaty 1 Feb 1662 | Wikipedia "Siege of Fort Zeelandia"; Britannica "Zheng Chenggong" |
| Qing took Taiwan in 1683; ceded to Japan in 1895; Japanese rule until 1945 | ✓ Treaty of Shimonoseki | Britannica "Taiwan"; Wikipedia "Taiwan under Japanese rule" |
| 1949 Nationalists under Chiang Kai-shek retreated to Taiwan; both claimed to be China | ✓ | Britannica; Wikipedia "Retreat of the government of the Republic of China to Taiwan" |
| 1950, Korean War: Truman sent the Seventh Fleet into the strait | ✓ 27 June 1950 | history.state.gov; Wikipedia |
| 1958: China shelled Kinmen for weeks; US ships and jets backed Taiwan | ✓ from 23 Aug 1958 (44 days of heavy shelling) | Wikipedia "Second Taiwan Strait Crisis"; history.state.gov |
| In 2022 nearly half the world's container ships passed through | ✓ ~48% of the global container fleet, Jan–Jul 2022 | Bloomberg (Aug 2022); Reuters |
| Taiwan makes about 90% of the most advanced chips | ✓ ~90%+ of leading-edge logic | US CRS / BCG–SIA report; Reuters |
| China claims Taiwan, has not ruled out force; frequent drills around the island | ✓ 2005 Anti-Secession Law; drills 2022–2026 | France 24; PBS |
