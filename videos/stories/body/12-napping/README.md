| Claim | Sources |
|---|---|

| Per extra hour of napping HR 1.13 (1.04–1.23); per extra daily nap HR 1.07 (1.02–1.13); morning vs early-afternoon nappers HR 1.30 (1.03–1.64); irregularity (variability) not associated, HR 1.01 | Neuroscience News (2026, with paper's table); ScienceDaily (Nov 2026); Medical News Today (2026) |
| Associations held after accounting for night-time sleep duration/fragmentation; naps got longer, more frequent and earlier with age | Medical News Today (2026); Neuroscience News (2026) |
| Lead author Chenlu Gao: "correlation, not causation"; naps may reflect underlying disease, sleep disturbance, circadian dysregulation | ScienceDaily (Nov 2026); Neuroscience News (2026) |
| Post-lunch dip is circadian, occurs even without lunch; worsened by high-carb meal | Monk TH, "The post-lunch dip in performance", *Clin Sports Med* 2005 (PMID 15892914); Sleep Foundation "Why do I get sleepy after eating"; Oxford NDPH blog |

| NASA (Rosekind et al., 1994–95): 40-min cockpit rest opportunity, fell asleep in 5.6 min, slept 25.8 min; benefits through descent/landing; up to 54% alertness, 34% performance | NASA Technical Memorandum (NTRS 19950006379); Sleep Foundation "NASA nap" |
| Experts suggest naps ~20 min / under 30 min, early afternoon | Sleep Foundation "NASA nap"; Sleep Foundation "Coffee nap" |
| Caffeine takes ~20 min to kick in; coffee then nap ≤20 min | National Geographic; Sleep Foundation "Coffee nap" |
| Reyner & Horne 1997: 12 sleepy drivers, simulator; caffeine + short nap cut incidents to 9% of placebo vs 34% caffeine alone | Reyner LA, Horne JA, *Psychophysiology* 1997 (PMID 9401427); SWOV publication record |

| Yamada et al. 2015 *Sleep* meta-analysis: naps ≥60 min CVD RR 1.82, all-cause mortality RR 1.27; <60 min not associated; J-curve, dip from 0–30 min | Yamada T et al., *Sleep* 2015 (PMC4667384); JAMA commentary on 2015 nap meta-analyses |
| ESC Congress 2020 meta-analysis (Pan et al.): 313,651 people, >20 studies; naps >60 min → 30% higher all-cause death, 34% higher CVD; risk mainly in those sleeping >6 h at night | ESC press release (Aug 2020); ScienceDaily / EurekAlert (26 Aug 2020) |
