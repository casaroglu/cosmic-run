# 06 — Cleopatra, the pyramids and the Moon landing: fact check

| Claim | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| She ruled Egypt until 30 BC | Died 10 or 12 Aug 30 BC, aged 39; born 70/69 BC ✓ | Wikipedia "Cleopatra" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleopatra); History.com "Cleopatra" (https://www.history.com/articles/cleopatra) |
| She didn't build the pyramids; Khufu's Great Pyramid | Built for Khufu, 4th Dynasty ✓ | Wikipedia "Great Pyramid of Giza" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Pyramid_of_Giza); National Geographic "Egypt's great pyramids…" (https://www.nationalgeographic.com/history/history-magazine/article/egypt-great-pyramids-giza-plateau) |
| Around 2560 BC | "c. 2600 BC" (Wikipedia; dating ranges 2613–2577 / 2620–2484 BC); work begins ~2550 BC (Nat Geo); 2560 BC (WorldAtlas) → script "around 2560 BC" | Wikipedia; National Geographic; WorldAtlas (https://www.worldatlas.com/articles/so-cleopatra-lived-closer-in-time-to-the-first-lunar-landing-than-the-great-pyramids.html) |
| Pyramid → Cleopatra about 2,500 years | 2560 BC → 69 BC = ~2,490; → 30 BC = ~2,530 ✓ (any pyramid date 2600–2550 BC gives 2,480–2,570) | Arithmetic from the rows above; WorldAtlas states "~2,500" |
| Cleopatra → Apollo 11 (1969) about 2,000 years | 30 BC → AD 1969 = 1,998 years (no year 0) ✓ | NASA "Apollo 11 Mission Overview" (https://www.nasa.gov/history/apollo-11-mission-overview/) for 20 July 1969; WorldAtlas "~2,000" |
| Closer to Armstrong's first step | Armstrong first on the surface, 20 July 1969 ✓ | NASA; Wikipedia "Apollo 11" |
| To her, the pyramid was older than she is to us | ~2,500 years (pyramid→her) > ~2,056 years (30 BC→2026) ✓ | Arithmetic from the rows above |
| Still stands at Giza, the last ancient Wonder | Only one of the Seven Wonders still standing ✓ | National Geographic; Wikipedia "Great Pyramid of Giza" |

**Dropped:** "tallest structure for 3,800 years" — Wikipedia text says "more than 3,700 years", infobox ~3,800, WorldAtlas says until the Eiffel Tower (1889). Sources disagree, not used. "Last pharaoh / Greek dynasty" line cut for length (Wikipedia: "last active Hellenistic pharaoh"; History.com: Macedonian dynasty founded by Ptolemy) — safe to restore if time allows.
