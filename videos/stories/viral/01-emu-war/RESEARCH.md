**Dropped (sources disagree):** kill count. Meredith claimed 986 kills with 9,860 rounds plus 2,500 wounded (Wikipedia, Popular Science — "highly disputed"); National Geographic says about 2,500 killed; Australian Geographic says roughly 2,500 rounds fired and 50–200 emus killed. No number of dead emus is used in the script.
