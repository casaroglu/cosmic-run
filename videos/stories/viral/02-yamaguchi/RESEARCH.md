# 02 — Tsutomu Yamaguchi, survivor of both atomic bombs: fact check

| Claim | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| One man survived both bombs: Tsutomu Yamaguchi | Only person *officially recognised* as surviving both bombings ✓ (wording: at least 160–165 people are known double survivors, so the script never says "only person") | Wikipedia "Tsutomu Yamaguchi" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsutomu_Yamaguchi); ABC News "Man Who Survived Hiroshima and Nagasaki Atomic Bombs Dies" (https://abcnews.com/WN/atomic-bomb-victim-survived-hiroshima-nagasaki-dies-japan/story?id=9489697) |
| August 1945, Mitsubishi engineer, aged 29, in Hiroshima on business | Mitsubishi Heavy Industries draftsman/engineer on a business trip; born 16 Mar 1916 → 29 ✓ | Wikipedia; RNZ "Tsutomu Yamaguchi: The man who survived both atomic bombs" (https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/top/569474/tsutomu-yamaguchi-the-man-who-survived-both-atomic-bombs); ABC News |
| About three kilometres from the blast | ~3 km from the Hiroshima hypocentre ✓ | Wikipedia; RNZ |
| Burned, temporarily blind and deaf | Serious burns, temporarily blinded, ruptured eardrums / temporarily deaf ✓ | Wikipedia; ABC News |
| His home was Nagasaki | Born and lived in Nagasaki ✓ | Wikipedia; RNZ |
| Went home the next day | Returned to Nagasaki the day after (Wikipedia; RNZ "after a night in a bomb shelter") ✓ — ABC says "three days later" he returned (it means the day of the bomb); script follows Wikipedia + RNZ | Wikipedia; RNZ |
| 9 August, bandaged, described Hiroshima to his boss | Reported to work 9 Aug in bandages; was describing the Hiroshima blast to his supervisor ✓ | Wikipedia; RNZ |
| The boss didn't believe him | "His boss doubted him" ✓ (RNZ); Wikipedia gives the supervisor scene without stating disbelief — reported anecdote | RNZ; Wikipedia |
| Nagasaki bomb also about three kilometres away; he survived | ~3 km (Wikipedia); "less than 2 miles" (ABC) ✓ | Wikipedia; ABC News |
| 2009: Japan officially recognised him for both | Government accepted double recognition March 2009 ✓ | Wikipedia; ABC News |
| Campaigned against nuclear weapons | Advocated abolition, incl. UN screening of *Twice Survived* (2006) ✓ | Wikipedia; RNZ |
| Died at 93 (2010) | 4 Jan 2010, Nagasaki, aged 93 ✓ | Wikipedia; ABC News |

**Softened:** "only survivor of both" → "survived both" / "officially recognised" (Wikipedia: at least 160 people affected by both bombs). Boss's disbelief rests mainly on RNZ.
