# 10 — London Beer Flood: fact check

| Claim | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| October 1814, Horse Shoe Brewery, central London | 17 Oct 1814, Meux & Co's Horse Shoe Brewery at Tottenham Court Road / Oxford St–Great Russell St corner ✓ | Wikipedia "London Beer Flood" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Beer_Flood); Historic UK "The London Beer Flood of 1814" (https://www.historic-uk.com/HistoryUK/HistoryofBritain/The-London-Beer-Flood-of-1814/); History.com "The London Beer Flood" (https://www.history.com/articles/london-beer-flood) |
| A vat 22 feet tall held about a million pints of porter | 22 ft wooden vat, 3,555 imperial barrels (≈1.02 million pints); History.com: "equivalent of 1 million pints" ✓. Total released, including other vessels it broke, is disputed (128,000–323,000 gallons) → script talks only about the one vat | Wikipedia; History.com; Historic UK (22 ft, 3,500+ barrels) |
| It burst | ✓ c. 5:30 pm | Wikipedia; History.com |
| Into St Giles, a packed slum | St Giles rookery ✓ | Wikipedia; History.com; Historic UK |
| A 15-foot wave knocked down walls and flooded basements | Wave c. 15 ft ✓; brewery wall collapsed; wake was held in a basement/cellar ✓ | Wikipedia; Historic UK; History.com (wall, cellar) |
| Eight died, including mourners at a wake | 8 dead incl. five (or four) mourners at a child's wake ✓. Historic UK repeats a "ninth death from alcohol poisoning"; Wikipedia (citing brewing historian Martyn Cornell) calls the drinking stories myths → not used | Wikipedia; History.com; Historic UK |
| A jury called it an act of God | Inquest 19 Oct 1814: "casually, accidentally and by misfortune", i.e. an Act of God; no compensation ✓ | History.com; Historic UK; Wikipedia |
| The brewery got its tax back | Excise duty on the lost beer refunded/waived (c. £7,250) ✓ | Wikipedia; History.com; Historic UK |
| The Dominion Theatre stands there now | ✓ (brewery demolished 1922; theatre partly occupies site) | Wikipedia; Historic UK |
| Title "London was flooded by beer" | A district (St Giles), not the whole city → script says "a wave of beer killed eight Londoners" | — |
