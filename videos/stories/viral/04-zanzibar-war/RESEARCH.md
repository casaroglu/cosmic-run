# 04 — Anglo-Zanzibar War (1896): fact check

| Claim | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Shortest war in history, about 38 minutes | 38 min (09:02–09:40) most cited; 40, 43 or 45 minutes in other sources → "about 38", later "sources say 38 to 45" ✓ | Wikipedia "Anglo-Zanzibar War" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Zanzibar_War); Historic UK "The Shortest War in History" (https://www.historic-uk.com/HistoryUK/HistoryofBritain/The-Shortest-War-in-History/); Historical Association (https://www.history.org.uk/student/categories/107/resource/7950); History Hit (https://www.historyhit.com/the-anglo-zanzibar-war-the-shortest-war-in-history-which-lasted-38-minutes/) |
| Zanzibar, East Africa, August 1896, British Empire vs new sultan | 27 Aug 1896; Khalid bin Barghash took power after Sultan Hamad died 25 Aug ✓ | Wikipedia; Historical Association |
| Khalid seized the throne without Britain's approval | ✓ | Wikipedia; Historic UK |
| British warships in the harbour | Royal Navy ships (five, per HA) in the harbour ✓ | Historical Association; South African History Online "Anglo Zanzibar war 1896" (https://www.sahistory.org.za/article/anglo-zanzibar-war-1896) |
| Leave by nine a.m.; he refused | Ultimatum to leave the palace by 09:00 on 27 Aug; refused ✓ | Wikipedia; Historic UK; SAHO |
| At nine, the ships opened fire | Bombardment from 09:00/09:02 ✓ | Wikipedia; Historic UK |
| Yacht Glasgow sunk (visual only) | HHS *Glasgow* sunk ✓ | Wikipedia; SAHO |
| Around nine forty, the flag came down | Shelling stopped ~09:40, flag cut down ✓ | Wikipedia; Historic UK |
| About 500 killed or wounded; one Briton hurt | ~500 Zanzibari casualties; one British petty officer/sailor (HA: marine) wounded ✓ | Wikipedia; Historic UK; Historical Association |
| Khalid fled to the German consulate | ✓ | Wikipedia; Historic UK; Historical Association; SAHO |
| Still called the shortest war on record | "Shortest in recorded history" (Guinness 2008 cited) ✓ | Wikipedia; SAHO |

**Softened:** duration ("about 38", "sources say 38 to 45"); Khalid called "his relative" avoided — Wikipedia says nephew, Historic UK says cousin, so the script only names Khalid. **Dropped:** "Britain made Zanzibar pay for the shells" — Wikipedia says Khalid's supporters paid 300,000 rupees, other sources don't mention it. Rumour that Khalid poisoned Hamad (Historic UK: never confirmed) not used.
