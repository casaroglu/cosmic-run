## Borders (sketch maps)
- **Start, AD 306:** the whole empire (post-Aurelian frontier, faint) and Constantine's share in blue: Britain, Gaul, Spain (with the Rhine armies). Markers York and Niš.
- **End, AD 337:** the whole empire in blue under one emperor (306 share faint), arrow Rome → Constantinople.
- Bosphorus map: Europe / Asia / Black Sea and Byzantium (Natural Earth coastline).
