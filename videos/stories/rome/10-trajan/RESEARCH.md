## Borders
- AD 117 polygon (`sketch` items, drawn by hand from the standard Trajanic maps, clipped to Natural Earth land): Britain to the Solway–Tyne line; Rhine, Upper German–Raetian limes and Danube; Dacia (with Muntenia/S. Moldavia under Moesia Inferior); Armenia, Mesopotamia, Assyria to the Gulf; Arabia Petraea to Hegra; Egypt to the First Cataract; North African coastal provinces.
- End map: AD 117 (faint) and after Hadrian (solid): eastern provinces and the lands north of the lower Danube east of the Olt given up. Borders are approximate; the caption says so.

## Left out on purpose
- "First emperor born outside Italy": commonly said but contested (Dio calls him Iberian; modern scholars see an Italian family in Spain). The script just says "born in Spain".
- Population of the empire (55–100 million estimates vary too much).
