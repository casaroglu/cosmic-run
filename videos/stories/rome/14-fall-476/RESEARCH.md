| Odoacer did not take the title of emperor; the regalia were sent to Zeno in Constantinople with the message that one emperor was enough for both halves | ✓ (Malchus fr. 14: "one shared emperor was sufficient for both territories"; the embassy was formally the Roman Senate's) | Wikipedia "Romulus Augustulus"; Wikipedia "Fall of the Western Roman Empire"; History Hit "Who Was Emperor Zeno" |

Left out on purpose: the exact age of Romulus; the 6,000-solidi figure (single ancient source); the place of Orestes' death; Theodoric (493).

## Borders (sketch maps, approximate)
- **Start, AD 475:** the Eastern Empire in blue (the post-Aurelian frontier east of the 395 division line: Drina – Sirmium/Singidunum, and the Gulf of Sirte between Tripolitania and Cyrenaica) and what was left of the Western Empire in red: Italy with Sicily (Sicily was mostly Vandal-held until the 477 treaty; drawn as Italy's). Kingdoms named without borders: Visigoths, Vandals, Franks. Markers Ravenna, Constantinople.
- **End, AD 476:** the same East in blue, "Odoacer's Italy" faint, kingdoms; "Nepos in Dalmatia" named on the Nepos line.
- Coastline: Natural Earth (via `~/cizgi/rome-13-constantine/refs/empire.json`). Division line hand-drawn after Shepherd, *Historical Atlas* (1911), "The Roman Empire about 395".
