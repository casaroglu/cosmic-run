Left out on purpose: the date debate (traditional 24 August vs. autumn, the 2018 charcoal inscription of 17 October; Wikipedia says the question is not settled), so the video only says "AD 79"; Herculaneum's story; death-toll estimates (1,150 bodies found at Pompeii; totals 1,500–16,000 are disputed).
