Left out on purpose: the exact number of murdered emperors (sources count claimants differently); Decius at Abritus (251); the Historia Augusta's giant-shepherd tales about Maximinus.

## Borders (sketch maps, approximate)
- **Start, AD 235:** frontier on Hadrian's Wall, Rhine, the upper German–Raetian limes (Agri Decumates inside), Danube with Dacia, eastern frontier with Mesopotamia (Nisibis, Singara, Dura), Arabia, Egypt, North Africa.
- **Split, AD 271:** Gallic Empire = Gaul + Britain (Spain back with Rome from c. 269); Palmyrene = Egypt, Syria, Arabia, Mesopotamia and Asia Minor east of about Ancyra (approximate line); Rome = the rest (faint 235 outline).
- **End, AD 284:** reunited empire without Dacia and the Agri Decumates (Rhine–Iller–Danube line), with the 235 outline faint for comparison.
- Rings in `episode.json` (generated from a frontier list: Rhine, limes, Danube, Dacia, eastern frontier); sea legs are clipped by the coastline fill. Map data: Natural Earth.
