# Ancient Rome 7: Augustus and the month of August: fact check

| Claim | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Born 23 Sept 63 BC; ~18 when Caesar died; Caesar's great-nephew, adopted in his will | ✓ | Wikipedia "Augustus"; Suetonius, *Divus Augustus* 5, 8 |
| Ruled ~40 years (27 BC–AD 14) | ✓ | Wikipedia "Augustus"; Britannica "Augustus" |
| Actium, 2 Sept 31 BC; Agrippa commanded; Antony and Cleopatra fled to Egypt and killed themselves (30 BC); Egypt annexed | ✓ | Wikipedia "Battle of Actium"; Wikipedia "Augustus"; Plutarch, *Antony* 66–86 |
| Claimed to restore the Republic; princeps "first citizen"; 16 Jan 27 BC Senate grants "Augustus" ("revered") | ✓ | Wikipedia "Augustus"; *Res Gestae* 34 |
| Sextilis renamed Augustus in 8 BC, because of his victories in that month (incl. Egypt) | ✓ | Wikipedia "August"; Macrobius, *Saturnalia* I.12.35 |
| "Day stolen from February" is a medieval myth (Sacrobosco); Sextilis already had 31 days | ✓ | Wikipedia "August"; Wikipedia "Julian calendar" |
| "Found Rome a city of brick, left it a city of marble"; start of the Pax Romana (~200 years) | ✓ | Suetonius, *Divus Augustus* 28; Wikipedia "Pax Romana" |
| Died 19 August AD 14 at Nola, aged 75; last words about playing his part / applause | ✓ | Wikipedia "Augustus"; Suetonius, *Divus Augustus* 99–100 |
