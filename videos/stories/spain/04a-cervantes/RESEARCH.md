# Episode 4 — The Golden Age, Part 1 (Cervantes): fact check

| Claim | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 24 at Lepanto, two shots in the chest, a third ruined his left hand | Baptised 9 Oct 1547 (Alcalá); Lepanto 7 Oct 1571; two arquebus shots in the chest, a third left the left hand useless ("el manco de Lepanto") ✓ | Centro Virtual Cervantes (Canavaggio biography); Britannica "Miguel de Cervantes" |
| Galley taken by pirates, 26 Sep 1575, off Catalonia | Galley *El Sol* seized by the corsair Arnaut Mamí off the Catalan coast, 26 Sep 1575 ✓ | Centro Virtual Cervantes; Google Arts & Culture "Cervantes: a life in the Golden Age" |
| Slave in Algiers ~5 years, four escape attempts, took the blame alone | ✓ (two by land, two by sea) | Centro Virtual Cervantes; Garcés, *Cervantes in Algiers* |
| Ransomed by Trinitarians, 19 Sep 1580 | ✓ | Centro Virtual Cervantes; EB1911 "Cervantes" |
| Tax collector; Seville jail 1597 | Commissary/tax collector in Andalusia 1587–1597; jailed in Seville 1597 over his accounts ✓ (cause varies by source: bank failure / judge Vallejo / discrepancies — script says only "trouble with his accounts") | Centro Virtual Cervantes; biografiasyvidas |
| Book "born in a prison" | Prologue to *Don Quixote* Part 1: "se engendró en una cárcel" ✓ (hinted, not stated as Seville) | *Don Quixote* (1605), prologue |
| Published 1605 in Madrid, a hit at once | Printed by Juan de la Cuesta for Francisco de Robles, Madrid 1605 (privilege 26 Sep 1604); several editions within the year ✓ | Britannica "Publication of Don Quixote"; Library of Congress catalogue |
| Died April 1616; Shakespeare "same day" is a calendar myth | Cervantes d. 22 Apr 1616, buried 23 Apr (Gregorian); Shakespeare d. 23 Apr 1616 Julian = 3 May Gregorian ✓ | Folger Shakespeare Library blog; OUP blog |
| No genuine portrait | The Jáuregui-attributed portrait (RAE) is not accepted as authentic ✓ | Centro Virtual Cervantes (attribution marked "?"); UAB BDHAH note |

## Left out on purpose
- Ransom amount (500 ducats vs 500 escudos vs 300 escudos — sources disagree).
- The exact cause of the 1597 imprisonment (contested).
- Exact January 1605 publication day (single weak source).
