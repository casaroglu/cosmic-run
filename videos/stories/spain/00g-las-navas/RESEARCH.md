## Left out on purpose
- Sancho VII and the chains of Navarre's coat of arms (lore, weak sources).
- Army sizes and casualty figures (medieval exaggerations).

## Dialogue remake (2026-11-16)
- Start map 1195: Christian north (Castile, Aragon, Navarre, Portugal) vs the Almohad south (frontier roughly Tagus/Guadiana south of Toledo; Valencia still Muslim) (✓ Wikipedia "Almohad Caliphate"; Britannica "Reconquista"). End map 1250: only the Emirate of Granada left; Córdoba 1236, Seville 1248 (✓ Wikipedia "Emirate of Granada"; Britannica "Reconquista"). Las Navas lies in the Sierra Morena (Jaén). Borders approx.
