# Prequel — Numantia: fact check

| Claim | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Celtiberian hill town near Soria; beat Rome for twenty years | Numantine War from 153 BC; Scipio sent after "20 years of hostilities" ✓ | Wikipedia "Siege of Numantia"; Numancia Soria "Scipio's siege" |
| Scipio Aemilianus, destroyer of Carthage (146 BC) | ✓ | Wikipedia "Siege of Numantia"; EBSCO "Celtiberian Wars" |
| A wall around the town, ~9 km, with towers; he waited | Circumvallation of c. 9 km with towers and camps ✓ | Numancia Soria; Wikipedia |
| Hunger, then disease | ✓ | Wikipedia; weaponsandwarfare.com |
| Many killed themselves rather than surrender | "most of the surviving Numantines committed suicide" (Appian's account) ✓ | Wikipedia; corvinus.nl "The Numantine War 134–133" |
| Scipio razed the town; agnomen Numantinus | ✓ | Wikipedia; EBSCO |
| Cervantes' tragedy *El cerco de Numancia*; "numantino" = resisting to the end | ✓ | Britannica "Miguel de Cervantes"; RAE dictionary "numantino" |

## Left out on purpose
- Length of the siege (sources give 8, 11 or 16 months).
- Number of towers / camps (accounts differ).
- Number of survivors.
