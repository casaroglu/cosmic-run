| Claim | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1895 Cuba rose against Spain again | Cuban War of Independence 1895–98 (after the Ten Years' War 1868–78) ✓ | Office of the Historian (history.state.gov) "The Spanish-American War, 1898"; Britannica "Spanish-American War" |

| War in April; Manila Bay 1 May — Pacific squadron destroyed within hours | ✓ | history.state.gov; Naval History and Heritage Command "Spanish-American War" |
| Santiago, 3 July: Atlantic fleet tried to break out; every ship lost | Cervera's squadron destroyed or beached ✓ | Naval History and Heritage Command; history.state.gov |
| Treaty of Paris, 10 Dec 1898: Spain gave up Cuba; Puerto Rico, Guam, Philippines to the US | Spain relinquished Cuba (not ceded to the US); ceded the rest, US paid $20 m ✓ | history.state.gov; HISTORY.com "Treaty of Paris ends Spanish-American War" |
| 1899: last Pacific islands sold to Germany | German–Spanish Treaty, Feb 1899: Carolines, Northern Marianas, Palau for 25 m pesetas ✓ | Wikipedia "German–Spanish Treaty (1899)"; The Spectator archive, 10 June 1899 |

## Left out on purpose
- The cause of the Maine explosion as fact (disputed).
- "Splendid little war" quote.
- Casualty numbers beyond the Maine.

## Image note
The treaty photo shows the **ratification** (Washington, 11 April 1899), not the December 1898 signing — the credit line says so.
