# Episode 2 — Charles V: fact check

| Claim in script | Fact | Check in |
|---|---|---|
| Born 24 Feb 1500, Ghent | ✓ | RAH DB~e "Carlos I", Britannica "Charles V" |
| Grandson of Isabella & Ferdinand | Mother Joanna was their daughter ✓ | Britannica |
| Inherited Spain, Netherlands, southern Italy, the Americas | Burgundian Netherlands (1506), Castile+Aragon incl. Naples/Sicily/Sardinia & the Indies (1516) ✓ | Barton, *A History of Spain*, ch. 4 |
| Joanna confined 46 years | Tordesillas 1509–1555 (death) = 46 yrs ✓ | RAH DB~e "Juana I" |
| King at 16, barely spoke Spanish | Proclaimed March 1516 (age 16); arrived 1517 speaking little Castilian ✓ | Elliott, *Imperial Spain* |
| Comuneros 1520–21, leaders beheaded | Villalar 23 Apr 1521; Padilla, Bravo, Maldonado executed 24 Apr ✓ | Britannica "Comuneros revolt" |
| Elected emperor 28 June 1519 (Frankfurt), Fugger loans paid electors | ✓ — Fuggers lent ~543,000 of ~850,000 florins | Britannica "Fugger family" |
| Luther at Worms, April 1521, refused to recant | 17–18 April 1521 ✓ | Britannica "Diet of Worms" |
| Aztec & Inca conquests under his reign | Tenochtitlan fell Aug 1521; Cuzco taken 1533 ✓ | Britannica |
| Sack of Rome 6 May 1527 by mutinous unpaid imperial troops | ✓ | Britannica "Sack of Rome" |
| Mühlberg 24 April 1547; Titian portrait 1548 | ✓ | Museo del Prado catalogue |
| Abdication 25 Oct 1555 Brussels, wept | Netherlands abdication; contemporary accounts describe him in tears, leaning on William of Orange ✓ | Elliott |
| Died 21 Sept 1558 at Yuste | ✓ | RAH DB~e |
| Philip inherited debts | Crown defaulted 1557 (first year of Philip's reign) ✓ | Barton |

Avoided on purpose: "empire on which the sun never set" (usually tied to Philip II), the Yuste mock-funeral legend (disputed).
