## Left out on purpose
- Total death toll (estimates range widely).
- Picasso's *Guernica* is not shown (© until 2043).
- Specific massacres (Paracuellos, Badajoz) — numbers disputed; both sides covered in one sentence.

## Image note
Posters are all from the Republican side (the only large PD set found); the Nationalist side is shown through the 1936 Burgos photos and the 1939 victory parade.
