# Seduction #1 — Mata Hari: facts, sources, images

## Facts (each checked against two sources)
- Born Margaretha Zelle, 7 Aug 1876, Leeuwarden, Netherlands; dancer in Paris from 1905 (Musée Guimet debut). — Britannica; History.com
- German consul Karl Kroemer paid her 20,000 francs and gave code name H-21 (WWI). — National Geographic; History.com
- She also offered/agreed to spy for France (Georges Ladoux) for a promised one million francs (never paid). — National Geographic; Chemins de mémoire (French Ministry of Armed Forces)
- Arrested in Paris 13 Feb 1917 (hotel name differs between sources: Elysée Palace / Plaza Athénée → script says only "in Paris"). — Britannica; DePaul Vincentian Collections
- Executed by firing squad at Vincennes, 15 Oct 1917. — Britannica; History.com
- Prosecutor André Mornet and investigator Pierre Bouchardon later admitted there was not enough evidence for the serious charges. — EBSCO Research Starters; National Geographic
